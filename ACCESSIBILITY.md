# Accessibility

This project aims to make its portfolio and application usable by as many people as possible. Accessibility is an ongoing part of design and development, including keyboard access, clear focus indicators, semantic page structure, readable contrast, and support for reduced-motion preferences.

The application includes interactive pages and third-party contact and booking integrations. Some content or functionality may not work equally well with every assistive technology, browser, or device, and this project does not claim full conformance with a particular accessibility standard.

## Report a barrier

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, email [guuleedmaxamuud40@gmail.com](mailto:guuleedmaxamuud40@gmail.com) with the page or feature, what you were trying to do, and (if you wish) your browser, device, or assistive technology. You can also open a GitHub issue without including sensitive personal or health information. The maintainer will use reports to investigate and improve the experience.
