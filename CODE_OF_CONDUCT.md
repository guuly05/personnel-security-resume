# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone participating in this project should be treated with respect. We welcome constructive, considerate participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give specific, helpful feedback focused on the work.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information without their consent.
- Keep discussion relevant and professional.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Sexualized language or attention is not appropriate in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy to [guuleedmaxamuud40@gmail.com](mailto:guuleedmaxamuud40@gmail.com). Include a description and any relevant links. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical. The project maintainer may edit or remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing this project.
